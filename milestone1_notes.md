# Milestone 1 — Community and candidate distinctions

**Community:** r/TrueCrime (public subreddit, covers many different real cases,
so no single case's details dominate or require prior context to follow)

**Candidate distinctions noticed while reading comments:**

1. Some comments cite specific, checkable facts (exact dates, named people,
   legal outcomes, case details) while others make a claim with nothing
   backing it up.
2. Some comments are pure emotional reaction ("living rent free in my head",
   "makes my blood boil") with no argument or claim attached, versus comments
   that are actually making a point.
3. Some comments are bare case-name drops with zero elaboration (just naming
   a case, nothing else), versus comments that give real context and detail.
4. Some comments cite an external source (a podcast, a book, a named
   investigator) to support what they're saying, versus comments that just
   state a belief with no sourcing at all.
